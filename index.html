About

Biography & Details

Academic profile, contact information, and professional summary.

Dr. Mohammed Aman Ullah is a Professor in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Chittagong, Bangladesh. He earned his Ph.D. in Mathematics from Shanghai University, China, specializing in numerical simulations of material interfaces using conservative front-tracking methods.

His research spans computational mathematics, computational fluid dynamics, interfacial instability, numerical methods, general theory of relativity, and mathematical biology. He has published in reputable journals including PLOS ONE, Springer Nature, and the Journal of Computational Physics: X.

Dr. Ullah has served as Chairman of the Department of Mathematics (2021–2024) and has been recognized with a Gold Medal and several scholarships for academic excellence.
